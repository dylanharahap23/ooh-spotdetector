Demonstration data. Site IDs are anonymous and positions are rounded to roughly 110 m. City and district names are real; nothing here is an address, a client name or a live site record. Photographs are representatives from field surveys with identifying details blurred.
Consumer figures follow a published model. Model sms-location-attractiveness-45-v1: consumer ≤ 25 + OSM road proxy ≤ 20 = /45, using points = allocation × (0.6 × proximity portion + 0.4 × min(count / count cap, 1)). Counts come from Foursquare Open Source Places (2025-02-06, Apache-2.0) over a 2 km disc; every row publishes its count, cap, nearest distance and portion so the score can be recomputed by hand. Each site links out to Google Maps — we never store Google content, only the link.
